# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming project for people of every background and level of
experience. Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique ideas and code,
not people. Respect privacy and accessibility needs, and accept good-faith
corrections.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, doxxing,
impersonation, spam and retaliation are not acceptable. Do not publish anyone's
personal information or captured activity without their explicit permission.
These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews and other spaces where someone represents this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock conversations,
warn participants, and temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
They should explain moderation decisions where doing so is safe, apply these
rules consistently, and avoid disclosing private reports or reporter identities.
Conflicts of interest should be disclosed and moderation handed to another
maintainer where one is available.

For harassment, threats or private information, use GitLab's **Report abuse**
action on the relevant content/profile. That reports privately to GitLab and is
not a private maintainer inbox. For non-sensitive moderation requests, open an
issue without repeating abusive or private material. Do not publish sensitive
details just to request moderation. Security vulnerabilities belong in the
private reporting flow described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

For minor first incidents, maintainers may explain the rule and request a
correction. Serious or repeated incidents may lead directly to a temporary or
permanent ban. Participants may request reconsideration in a non-sensitive
moderation issue; continued harassment is not an appeal.
